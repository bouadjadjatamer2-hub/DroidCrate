## No bundled vendor tools

DroidCrate does not bundle, mirror, patch, crack, or redistribute closed-source
vendor tools. It stores names, descriptions, compatibility notes, and links to
official vendor pages or official open-source repositories. Any proprietary tool
must be obtained from its owner under the owner's terms.

The only executable downloaded by the app is Google's Android SDK Platform-Tools,
from Google's official `dl.google.com` endpoint. The download is checked against
a SHA-256 digest before it is unpacked, and the app does not redistribute it.

## No firmware

The project contains no firmware images, bootloader unlock codes, bypass tools, or
instructions for circumventing ownership or account protections. It is a learning
and documentation aid for devices you own or are authorised to service.
